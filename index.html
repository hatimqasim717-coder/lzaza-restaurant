Our Story

A family-style kitchen, built on Eid Gah Road.

Lzaza — meaning "deliciousness" — has been a Gojra gathering spot for family dinners, friend hangouts and celebrations alike. Our kitchen runs three menus side by side: classic Pakistani karahis and BBQ off the coals, and wok-fired Chinese plates, all cooked to order.
